About this meeting

Never Alone is our longest-running Toronto meeting and a good first meeting for anyone new. The hour opens with a reading from Life with Hope, followed by open sharing on a topic chosen by the group. No one is ever required to speak.

This is an open meeting — family, friends, and anyone curious about the program are welcome to attend alongside members. We finish by 8:30 PM, and a few of us usually stay for coffee nearby afterwards.
